Tickets, trains and seats

  • MetriGo tickets, QR codes and PDFs are for demonstration only. They will not open Delhi Metro gates and are not valid for travel. Buy real tickets from DMRC.
  • Routes use approximate station positions on the Yellow and Blue lines only. Times, live train positions, crowd levels and other passengers' seats are generated by a simulation, not taken from DMRC.
  • Delhi Metro has no reserved seating. Seat selection is a feature of this demo.

Prices and payments

  • Every price is calculated by the server and shown before you pay: a distance-based fare, multiplied by the time-of-day rate shown with each train, times the number of seats. Paying with the Metro Card takes 10% off. There are no booking or convenience fees.
  • Payments run in test mode. No money is taken and Metro Card balances have no cash value.
  • Cancellations and refunds follow the refund policy.
