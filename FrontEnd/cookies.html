Cookie policy

Last updated 16 October 2026

Summary

MetriGo uses one cookie, to keep you signed in, and stores a few settings on your device. All of it is needed for the app to work or remembers a choice you made. There are no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, so there is nothing to opt out of.

Cookie

NamePurposeLasts
connect.sidKeeps you signed in. HTTP-only, so page scripts can't read it.24 hours, or until you log out

NamePurposeLasts

mg-install-dismissedRemembers you closed the “Install MetriGo” promptUntil you clear site data

mg-me (session storage)Your profile, so pages paint without waitingUntil you close the tab
metrigo (IndexedDB)Your upcoming tickets, so they open without signalCleared when you log out
App cache (mg-shell, mg-runtime, mg-api)Lets the app open offlineAccount data cleared when you log out

You can clear all of this in your browser's site settings. You'll be signed out and your settings will reset.

Third parties

Maps load images from OpenStreetMap, which sees your IP address but sets no cookies through MetriGo. Nothing else is loaded from other sites. More in the privacy policy.
