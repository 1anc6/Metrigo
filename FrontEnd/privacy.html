Privacy policy

Last updated 16 October 2026

MetriGo is a student demo

MetriGo is a student portfolio project, built and run by one developer (1anc6 on GitHub). It is not a registered business and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Delhi Metro Rail Corporation (DMRC).

Tickets made in MetriGo are not valid for travel. Timetables, train positions, crowd levels and seat occupancy are simulated. Payments run in test mode: no real money is taken.

We don't ask for your phone number, date of birth, address or ID. We never see full card or bank details: those would be entered in Razorpay's window, and we only receive a payment ID.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, analytics or tracking scripts, and no selling or sharing of your data for marketing.
  • No marketing email. The only email MetriGo sends is the password reset code you ask for.
  • No profiling of children, and no targeted content of any kind.

Who else handles your data

  • Vercel hosts the website and API, and Turso hosts the database. Both may process data outside India.
  • OpenStreetMap serves the map images. When a map loads, your browser asks OpenStreetMap's servers for them, which shows them your IP address. See the OSMF privacy policy.
  • Razorpay would process live payments, but this demo runs in test mode and does not contact Razorpay.
  • An email provider delivers password reset codes when one is configured.

Fonts and code libraries are served from MetriGo itself, so loading a page doesn't contact Google Fonts or other CDNs.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can:

  • See your data: your profile, trips and Metro Card pages show everything stored about you. Ask through support for a copy.
  • Correct it: change your name in Profile → Edit account information.
  • Erase it: Profile → Delete account removes it straight away (details below).
  • Withdraw consent: deleting your account withdraws it.
  • Raise a grievance or nominate someone to act for you: contact us (below). We aim to reply within 30 days.

Deleting your account

Profile → Delete account asks for your password and deletes your profile, saved stations, Metro Card history, support messages, pending checkouts and every signed-in session. Upcoming trips are cancelled so their seats are freed.

Past bookings are kept without your email or name, because seat and payment records point to them. They can no longer be linked to you. Tickets and data saved on your device are cleared when you're signed out.

Children

You must be 18 or older to create an account, or have a parent or guardian agree to you using MetriGo. We don't knowingly collect data from children without that agreement. If you think a child has signed up without it, contact us and we'll delete the account.

Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt, sessions are stored in the database with secure, HTTP-only cookies, and sign-in and reset requests are rate-limited. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't reuse a password from another service.

Contact

For privacy questions, requests and grievances:

  • Signed in: Help & Support → Send a message. Your message is stored with your account.
  • Not signed in: open an issue at github.com/1anc6/Metrigo/issues. Issues are public, so don't include personal details; ask for a private way to reach you instead.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too, and sign-up records which version you accepted. If MetriGo ever takes real payments or collects more data, this policy will be updated before that happens.
