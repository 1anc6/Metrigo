Refund policy

Last updated 16 October 2026

MetriGo is a student demo

MetriGo is a student portfolio project, built and run by one developer (1anc6 on GitHub). It is not a registered business and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Delhi Metro Rail Corporation (DMRC).

Tickets made in MetriGo are not valid for travel. Timetables, train positions, crowd levels and seat occupancy are simulated. Payments run in test mode: no real money is taken, so none is refunded either. This policy describes how the app behaves.

Cancelling a trip

  • Open the trip under Trips and tap Cancel trip any time before the train departs.
  • You get the full amount you paid back. There is no cancellation fee.
  • The refund goes to your Metro Card straight away, whichever way you paid.
  • After departure a trip can't be cancelled or refunded.

If your seats go while you pay

Seats are held for 15 minutes while you pay. If they are taken anyway before your payment completes, no ticket is issued and:

  • Metro Card and test-mode payments are not charged.
  • A live UPI, card or net banking payment would be refunded automatically to the original payment method, usually within 5–7 working days.

Metro Card balance

Metro Card top-ups are test payments, so the balance has no cash value and can't be withdrawn. Deleting your account removes the balance.

The price you see is the price you pay

The total on the checkout screen is the final amount: base fare × time-of-day rate × seats, minus 10% when paying with the Metro Card. Nothing is added after you tap Pay.

Questions

Use the topic “Refund request” when you contact us:

  • Signed in: Help & Support → Send a message. Your message is stored with your account.
  • Not signed in: open an issue at github.com/1anc6/Metrigo/issues. Issues are public, so don't include personal details; ask for a private way to reach you instead.
